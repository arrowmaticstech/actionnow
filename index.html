Features

Everything Your Agent Can Do

ActionNow is packed with powerful capabilities to keep you informed without lifting a finger.

Auto Join Groups

ActionNow automatically joins the WhatsApp groups you specify via invite links. No manual setup needed.

Reads Text Messages

Scans every text message in real-time, looking for keywords, phrases, or patterns you define.

Transcribes Audio

Voice notes are automatically transcribed to text using AI, so nothing slips through the cracks.

Analyzes Images

Uses AI vision to understand image content — screenshots, documents, photos — and extracts relevant info.

Smart Reporting

Sends concise, actionable reports directly to your WhatsApp number at intervals you choose.

Privacy First

End-to-end encrypted. Only the boss receives reports. Your data stays private and secure.
